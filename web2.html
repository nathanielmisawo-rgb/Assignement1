Web 2.0

Web 2.0 refers to the second generation of the World Wide Web, characterized by user-generated content, social media, and increased interactivity. It represents a shift from static web pages to dynamic, collaborative, and participatory web experiences.

Web 2.0 introduced features such as blogs, wikis, social networking sites, and multimedia sharing platforms. It empowered users to create and share content, connect with others, and participate in online communities.
