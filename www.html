World Wide Web (WWW)

The World Wide Web (WWW) is an information system where documents and other web resources are identified by URLs, interlinked by hypertext links, and accessible via the Internet.

The WWW was invented by Tim Berners-Lee in 1989 and has since become a fundamental part of modern life, enabling the sharing of information and communication on a global scale.
