Web 1.0

Web 1.0 refers to the early stage of the World Wide Web, characterized by static HTML pages and limited user interaction. During this phase, websites were primarily used to display information, and users were passive consumers of content.

Web 1.0 was marked by the absence of dynamic content, social media, and user-generated content. It was a read-only web where users could only view information without contributing or interacting with it.

The focus of Web 1.0 was on providing information and establishing an online presence for businesses and individuals. It laid the foundation for the development of the more interactive and dynamic Web 2.0 that followed.
