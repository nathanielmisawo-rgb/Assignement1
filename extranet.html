Extranet

An extranet is a private network that uses Internet protocols and infrastructure to share information and resources between organizations. It facilitates secure communication and data exchange between different entities.

An extranet allows organizations to extend their intranets to include external partners, suppliers, or customers, enabling them to collaborate and share information securely.
