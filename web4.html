Web 4.0

Web 4.0 refers to the fourth generation of the World Wide Web, characterized by the integration of advanced technologies such as the Internet of Things (IoT), artificial intelligence, and blockchain. It aims to create a more immersive and intelligent web experience by enabling seamless connectivity and interaction between devices and systems.

Web 4.0 is expected to enable new applications and services that leverage the power of connected devices and intelligent systems, such as smart homes, autonomous vehicles, and personalized healthcare.
