Intranet

An intranet is a private network that uses Internet protocol suite (TCP/IP) technologies to share information, operational systems, and other business services within an organization.

Intranets are commonly used by organizations to facilitate communication and collaboration among employees, providing a centralized platform for sharing resources and information.
